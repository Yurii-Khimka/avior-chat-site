Private Messenger

Communication you can actually trust.

Avior Chat is a private messenger for people who believe their conversations deserve respect — not tracking, not analysis, not uncertainty.

Avior Chat collects nothing, stores nothing, and reads nothing. The conversation belongs to the two people having it. That is the entire product.

Why it matters

The world changed. Communication should too.

People today face real, everyday challenges with the tools they use to talk. Not dramatic threats — just a quiet erosion of trust.

01

Data leaks happen quietly

Personal conversations end up in the wrong hands, not from hacking, but from services that treat your data as a resource.

02

Platforms collect more than needed

Most messengers track behaviour, build profiles, and make decisions about your data that you never agreed to.

03

Trust is hard to find

People genuinely don't know which apps are safe. Uncertainty has become a normal part of digital communication.

Our approach

Built on three simple principles.

Principle 01

Privacy by design

Privacy is not a feature we added later. It is the foundation the entire app is built on. Your data belongs to you — full stop.

Principle 02

Clarity, not complexity

You should not need to be a security expert to feel safe. Avior Chat handles the technical side — you just have a conversation.

Principle 03

Responsibility first

We are thoughtful about every decision we make: what we collect, what we store, what we ask of you. No surprises, ever.

How it works

Simple to use. Serious about privacy.

End-to-end encrypted

Every message is encrypted before it leaves your device. Only you and the people you write to can read it.

Small trusted circles

Designed for people who communicate with intention — family, close friends, trusted colleagues.

No ads. No tracking.

We do not show ads, build profiles, or sell data. Our business model is straightforward and honest.

Minimal and calm

A clean, distraction-free interface that gets out of your way.

Who it is for

For people who value thoughtful communication.

Avior Chat is not for everyone, and that is intentional. It is for people who have grown tired of feeling like a product — who want a place to talk that feels human, calm, and genuinely safe. Whether you are a parent, a professional, or simply someone who thinks carefully about the tools they use — this is built for you.
