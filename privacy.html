Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 May 2026

The short version: we collect as little as possible, we do not sell your data, and your messages are never readable by us.

What we collect

Blackbox Chat does not require an account, email address, phone number, or any personal information to use. The only operational data involved in running the service is:

  • Transient room codes that exist only while a chat session is active, used solely to pair two participants.
  • Encrypted message traffic relayed between paired peers during an active session. The server cannot read this traffic.
  • On your device only: locally cached preferences such as your chosen theme and activation status. These never leave your device.

What we do not collect

We have designed Blackbox Chat to avoid collecting data in the first place. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Message content — all messages are end-to-end encrypted and unreadable by the server.
  • Contact lists, address books, or social graphs.
  • Location data or device GPS information.
  • Behavioural data, usage analytics, or interaction metrics.
  • IP address logs — the server does not record IP addresses beyond what is required at the TCP layer to maintain a live connection.
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels.
  • Your encryption phrase — it never leaves your device and is used only as input to local key derivation.

How your data is used

The minimal operational data described above is used exclusively to deliver messages between two paired peers during an active session. Nothing is used for marketing, profiling, advertising, or sale to any party.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third-party analytics providers. The relay server is hosted on standard cloud infrastructure. The infrastructure provider sees only encrypted network traffic — they do not have access to message content, room metadata, or any personally identifiable information.

Data retention

Messages are never stored. They are relayed in real time between participants and exist only in transit. Rooms are destroyed the moment either participant disconnects. There is no chat history, no message archive, and no backup.

Locally stored preferences on your device (theme choice and activation status) persist until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personally identifiable information, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. You are in full control of the only data that exists — the local preferences stored on your device, which you can remove at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how Blackbox Chat handles data, please reach out through our support page or email us at sayhi.blackbox.chat@gmail.com.
